How I work with AI

I embrace AI-assisted development. I also believe you have to know the foundations to use it well.

Understanding how software actually works is what lets you steer AI instead of just accepting what it gives you. The best results come from collaboration: a developer who knows the fundamentals, working with AI to build services that are good, secure and expandable.
